The leader groups

Almost everything hangs off Space. Learn these ten letters and the rest follows: f find, l LSP, c code, r refactor/rename, g git hunk, h harpoon, d debug, t toggle, w window, X Trouble, a AI, p sessions.

Press Space and pause — which-key shows what's available, so you never have to recall a full sequence cold.

Selecting & jumping by syntax

How text objects read

Every one of these follows an operator. a means around (includes the delimiters), i means inside. So daf deletes a whole function, cif clears just its body, and vaf selects one.

Lookahead is on: if the cursor isn't inside one yet, it jumps forward to the next match.

Two quirks worth remembering

In LaTeX files, Spcl belongs to VimTeX. Your local-leader is also Space, so VimTeX claims 22 buffer-local Spcl… mappings there — ll compiles, lv views. They shadow the LSP group for that buffer only.

Copilot needs the proxy on a non-RU exit. Without it the server returns nothing and ghost text just never appears — no error, no warning.

No mapping matches that.
